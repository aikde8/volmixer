# Changelog

## v1.0.1 — 21 October 2026
- Bluetooth devices: a brand-new device now gets a Bluetooth rune icon with a "?" badge. Clicking it opens a "what is it" menu: Headset / headphones, TWS earbuds, Compact speaker, Speaker with subwoofer. The answer picks the icon (over-ear headphones, earbuds, a JBL-style cylinder — custom SVG, Icons8 subwoofer box) and the behaviour: headset and TWS go through the usual headset logic; speakers only take over the speakers ("Logic → When a speaker is connected", enabled by default), the applet does not touch their microphone or volume; without an answer, automation leaves the device alone, and once answered the connection logic runs right away. The Bluetooth class is not a reliable source — the Tronsmart Element T6 Plus advertises itself as a headset with a Handsfree microphone. The answer is shared between the device's sink and source (keyed on the card).
- New "Devices" settings page: types of every known Bluetooth device (including currently disconnected ones), policy for a new device — ask / use the Bluetooth class / treat as a headset. "Device labels" and "Show virtual devices" moved here as well. The "Logic" page got a new icon.
- The scroll wheel over the panel icon, the icon itself and its tooltip now follow the default sink (the one with the mixer checkmark), not plasma-pa's "preferred" sink. The preferred sink picks the first running sink, so after a speaker connected the wheel started controlling it instead of the chosen device. Step, 150 % ceiling, OSD and confirmation sound match the stock volume keys (still driven by kded under plasma-pa's rules).
- "Logic", setting "During a call, when a Bluetooth microphone is selected: switch the device into headset mode" (on by default): when active recording streams exist and the user picks a microphone belonging to a Bluetooth device whose card is in A2DP (no microphone there), the card is switched to the best headset profile by PipeWire priority (LC3 > mSBC > CVSD), and streams from the previous microphone — including those pinned to it — are moved to the new one. Outside a call (just music) nothing happens; WirePlumber returns the card to A2DP by itself. Default-device changes caused by the headset logic itself do not count.
- Disconnect logic: fall-back speakers / microphone are restored only if the device that went away was the current default (previously, unplugging any headset or speaker could pull the defaults away from every other device — for example from FreeBuds to the built-in output).
- The logic became more robust across plasmashell restarts: initial model population no longer counts as device connections (the logic waits 2 s with no new devices instead of a fixed 3 s after startup); on shutdown the logic turns itself off so that the defaults are not touched during a restart.
- The scroll wheel (and ↑/↓ keys) over a speaker slider in the mixer now play the volume-confirmation sound, same as when dragging the handle — one per burst of events, 150 ms after the last one.
- "Logic": the setting "When speakers change: pause media" was renamed to "When the audio source changes".
- "Logic", setting "Pin the browser recording stream to": the browser's recording stream (Yandex, Chrome, Firefox… matched by process name) is moved to the chosen microphone when the stream appears, when a device appears, and when the setting changes. The point: dead broadcast tabs keep a microphone recording stream open and WirePlumber refuses to drop the headset out of HFP back into A2DP while it is alive. Off by default.
- Device icons and the volumetric mute-button icon are pure silver, un-tinted; the `*-accent.png` layers are gone.
- Setting "Accent colour" (previously "Device icon accent") — marks the default device (checkmark, halo) and lights the mute-button LED. Default is now dark green `#1e7b3c` instead of purple.
- Setting "LED blinking" for the LED mute button: don't blink, blink red while muted, or pulse with the signal (brighter when there's sound, dim during silence). Default: blink while muted.
- Colour picking in the settings uses the regular KDE dialog (colour square + palette of basic colours) instead of the Qt Quick dialog, which in plasmashell on Qt 6.10 shows a blank field instead of the colour square.
- "Notifications" group to the left of the microphones: the "Notification sounds" slider (volume and mute for the system event role, same as in "System Settings → Sound"), icon is a silver bell from Icons8 3D Fluency. No signal level.
- The "muted" emblem (cross) is no longer drawn on top of the icon — the mute button at the bottom already shows the state.
- "Logic", setting "When speakers change: pause media": when the headset is disconnected (audio would otherwise fall back to the speakers) or connects and takes over the speakers, currently playing MPRIS players (browsers, Elisa and others) are paused. The pause happens immediately, before the switch. Off by default.
- WirePlumber 0.5's own "pause players when their device goes away" (`linking.pause-playback`) is turned off with --save on startup, so only this setting controls pausing. To restore: `wpctl settings --save linking.pause-playback true` (with the applet uninstalled).
